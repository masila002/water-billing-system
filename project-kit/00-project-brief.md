# MajiBill brief — MAJIBILL, the real product

**MajiBill** (Swahili: maji = water) is the multitenant water-billing
platform owned by 404HubSpot. Utilities (tenants) bill customers,
collect through M-Pesa/cash/bank, reconcile in a double-entry ledger,
and report to managers and regulators. Owner: 404HubSpot, Nakuru.

Audience: billing clerk, meter reader, cashier, accountant, utility
admin, auditor, reseller partner. Success: a pilot utility runs its
full month — onboarding → readings (offline) → bills → collections →
reports — with every shilling traceable and every action audited.

`demo-app/` is the pitch demo, not the product. This kit describes
MajiBill in root: `apps/api`, `apps/web`, `apps/reader`, `packages/`,
`db/`, `infra/`, `docs/`.
